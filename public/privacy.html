easyApply Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026 · Applies to easyApply version 1.0.0

easyApply helps you save job application information, review and fill supported forms, and keep an application tracker. Your saved information is stored locally in the Chrome profile where you use the extension. easyApply has no developer-operated backend, user accounts, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, or payment service.

Information you choose to save

You may enter names, email addresses, phone numbers, date of birth, gender, addresses, education, employment history, skills, projects, links, salary and work preferences, application answers, and eligibility information. Custom saved entries contain titles and values you provide. The tracker contains company, position, application URL, date, follow-up date, status, a text reference to the resume used, and notes.

easyApply also saves your selected profile and welcome-tour progress. Ordinary profiles, custom entries, tracker records, and preferences use chrome.storage.local. They are not encrypted by easyApply. The extension does not use Chrome Sync.

Optional remembered field answers

After you activate easyApply on a page, it can notice manually entered answers to supported unmatched questions and offer Save / Dismiss after you leave a field, without submitting. If you enable “Learn new answers automatically” for a profile, new ordinary answers are saved locally as you finish fields. Changed answers still require review. A pending suggestion is held temporarily in extension session storage so it can be shown again when easyApply is activated after navigation. Suggestions expire after 15 minutes and expired records are removed when next checked. With automatic learning off, it is not added to your saved profile unless you click Save. Dismiss removes the pending suggestion. Browser restart clears session suggestions. Saved answers use ordinary, unencrypted local profile storage, are included in profile exports, and can be edited or forgotten in Profile Settings. Matching uses normalized exact question text on your device, not an AI service. The extension does not request new persistent website access or submit forms for you.

Optional voluntary disclosures

You may explicitly enable user-approved gender, race/ethnicity, protected veteran status, and disability status defaults per profile. Choices start blank and autofill is off by default. These answers are stored with ordinary profiles in unencrypted local extension storage and included in profile exports. They are not sent as structured career facts to the AI provider. When enabled, Scan previews and Quick Fill can use saved answers on clearly matching, empty supported controls. Existing answers are preserved. Disable the option to stop future disclosure autofill; clear individual choices or delete the profile to remove saved answers. Disabling does not remove data already filled into a website.

Encrypted PAN and Aadhaar vault

PAN and Aadhaar entered in the dedicated identity vault are stored separately for each profile and encrypted locally using AES-GCM with a 256-bit key. A passphrase of at least 12 characters derives the key using PBKDF2-SHA256 with 310,000 iterations and a fresh salt. Each encryption uses a fresh initialization vector; new vault records are cryptographically bound to their profile.

Your vault passphrase and encryption key are not saved. Unlocked identity is held temporarily in memory and cleared from the popup after two minutes, after filling, when you lock it, or when the popup closes. Scan snapshots expire after two minutes or are consumed by filling. Closing the popup does not erase information already filled into a website. Forgotten passphrases cannot be recovered by the developer.

The Saved accounts section stores email/password pairs locally using AES-GCM encryption and a non-exportable device key in extension IndexedDB. Records load automatically without a passphrase. This does not protect against someone controlling your browser profile or device. Eye buttons reveal individual passwords; they hide when the window loses focus or after saving. Accounts are shared across job profiles, excluded from profile exports and AI requests, and are not used for automatic form filling. Remove a card and save to delete it. Older passphrase-protected account records are retained separately and are not automatically decrypted.

This identity protection applies only to the dedicated identity vault. Do not place identity numbers in ordinary notes or custom entries. Use only the separate encrypted Saved accounts section for login passwords; do not put them in ordinary profiles, notes, or custom entries. Do not store one-time codes, UPI PINs, or banking credentials.

Information processed on application pages

When you invoke scanning, Quick Fill, or the in-page panel, easyApply accesses the current page to find supported form controls. It processes labels, field attributes, existing field values, the page URL, and form actions to match fields and check whether a preview is still valid. It does not collect a browsing-history log.

The extension's injected code receives normal profile data through extension messaging when needed. Direct access to local storage is restricted to trusted extension contexts. The matching and filling operations run on your device.

The popup lets you review individual fields before filling. Quick Fill immediately fills eligible empty fields using the selected saved profile. Identity and protected eligibility answers are not automatically selected by Quick Fill. PAN and Aadhaar require unlocking, explicit selection, and confirmation through the popup; filling them on remote websites requires HTTPS.

Sharing and third-party services

easyApply does not send saved data, page content, or usage events to the developer or an analytics service. It does not sell, rent, or use your data for advertising.

When you fill information into a website, that website can read it immediately, even before you submit the form. Its privacy practices and any autosave behavior are outside easyApply's control. easyApply never submits applications itself.

Copy and text-download actions place ordinary saved information in your clipboard or a plain-text file at your request. Vault contents are excluded. You control where you paste or share exports. The Smart fill prompt feature copies text for you to use elsewhere; it does not upload a resume or call an AI service. If you separately send a resume or information to ChatGPT or another service, that service's policies apply.

If you save a resume, its file is stored locally in extension IndexedDB and can be removed from the resume card. It may be uploaded to supported application fields when you scan an application form or invoke resume filling. Scanning automatically attaches the selected profile’s saved resume to identified empty Resume/CV upload fields. It is not sent to the AI provider.

Local resume review and job suggestions

Resume Autofill reads PDF or TXT files on your device and previews extracted details before you apply them. Existing profile values are not selected for replacement by default. The resume file is saved only when you apply with the Resume Vault option enabled. Scanned image-only PDFs may require manual entry. Job-specific suggestions compare saved profile roles and skills with the job title and description locally; they do not analyze resume file contents or send the job description to an AI service.

Multi-step applications and tracking

After activation, easyApply may detect a Next or Continue action and briefly retain form-field labels, the current origin, and an expiry time in extension session storage to offer filling on the next step. The watch expires after 30 seconds, stays on the same origin, and does not automatically fill or submit the next step. Submission detection can create an application record from the page title, company, role, URL and date. Detection is not proof that an employer received the application; verify the confirmation yourself. Tracker follow-up dates are local records, not scheduled notifications.

Optional AI answers (Groq)

AI is disabled until you provide your own Groq API key, consent to sharing, and grant optional access to api.groq.com. When you choose Generate drafts, Generate & fill, or Scan application form with AI filling checked and AI enabled, selected career profile fields, your target role, company, job description and the displayed questions are sent directly to Groq to generate answers. Regenerating a single answer also sends that draft and your optional writing preference to Groq. These requests do not go through a developer server. Dedicated identity vault data, resume files, and structured contact and demographic fields are excluded. Career free text can still contain personal information; review the data preview before generating. Basic redaction is not a guarantee of anonymity.

By default, the API key is kept in trusted extension session storage and cleared when the browser session ends. If you enable Keep API key across browser restarts, an AES-GCM encrypted copy is saved in trusted local extension storage, with a non-exportable encryption key stored in extension IndexedDB. This allows automatic restoration after restarting Chrome. This is not password-protected or OS credential-vault storage and does not protect against someone controlling your device or browser profile. Turning persistence off deletes the saved copy; Disable AI removes the persistent and session copies. It is sent only to Groq for authentication. Answers remain in the open AI window until copied or filled. Groq account quotas, pricing and data-handling policies apply. Provider processing and any retention exceptions are outside easyApply's control. Deleting local information does not delete provider-held data.

Permissions

  • Optional https://api.groq.com/*: call Groq only when you enable AI and request generation.
  • storage: save and retrieve local profiles, vaults, tracker entries, custom entries, and preferences.
  • activeTab: temporarily access the active page after you invoke the extension.
  • scripting: inject code to scan and fill supported controls and display the in-page panel.
  • contextMenus: provide right-click Quick Fill and panel actions.

The default Quick Fill keyboard shortcut is registered through Chrome's commands feature. easyApply does not request permanent access to all production websites.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Saved information remains in your Chrome profile until you edit or delete it, remove the extension, or clear its local data. You can delete profiles, custom entries, tracker records, and identity vaults in the extension. Deleting a profile also removes its saved resume file, encrypted identity vault, and custom entries; tracker records are separate and must be deleted separately.

Deleting local information does not remove clipboard contents, exported files, information already sent to websites, or copies made by device backup software. Manage those copies separately. There is no developer-held account or cloud copy to delete or recover.

Limited use

easyApply uses user data only to provide the application assistance features described here. Its use and transfer of user data adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. User data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to determine creditworthiness.

Security and policy updates

Local storage is not an operating-system secure enclave. Protect your device and Chrome profile. Encryption and field checks reduce risk but cannot guarantee the safety of arbitrary websites or eliminate incorrect matches. Review filled applications before submitting.

Changes to these practices will be reflected in an updated policy and its date. Review the policy when updating the extension.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact or support channel on the easyApply Chrome Web Store listing from which you installed the extension. If you received an unpacked copy, contact its distributor. Do not include passphrases, identity numbers, or other secrets in a support request.
